# EU Job Intelligence Wiki

Welcome! This wiki explains the project in simple language.

## What is this?

A job-search assistant for European cloud roles that runs on your own computer.
It finds jobs, scores them against your profile, checks visa and salary information,
and helps you prepare applications. **You always make the final decision.**
